## Develop

- **Raws whose tone comes from the camera's JPEG keep its midtones and shadows:** the tone curve fitted to a raw's embedded camera JPEG is smoothed so it has no kinks (which posterized skin on some Sony bodies), and the smoothing lowered the curve through the shadows and midtones by up to a tenth of a stop, so such raws rendered darker than the camera and Lightroom (mid-grey 0.458 instead of the fitted 0.491 on a Canon EOS R5 C). The smoothed curve now keeps the fitted level along its whole length, without bringing the kinks back. On Lightroom's exports of eight R5 C test raws the default render is 1.5 levels closer on held-out shots (7.5 instead of 9.0 RMS).
