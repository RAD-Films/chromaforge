## Develop

- **Raws whose tone comes from the camera's JPEG keep its midtones:** the tone curve fitted to a raw's embedded camera JPEG is smoothed so it has no kinks (which posterized skin on some Sony bodies), and the smoothing lowered the middle of the curve by about a tenth of a stop, so such raws rendered darker than the camera and Lightroom (mid-grey 0.458 instead of the fitted 0.491 on a Canon EOS R5 C). The smoothed curve now keeps the fitted level through the midtones, without bringing the kinks back. On Lightroom's exports of eight R5 C test raws the default render is 1.1 levels closer on held-out shots (7.9 instead of 9.0 RMS). The deepest shadows still render a little darker than Lightroom.
