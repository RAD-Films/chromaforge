## Develop

- **Camera profiles follow the white balance (process V9):** on a raw with a camera calibration or an imported camera profile (`.dcp`), or a DNG with its own profile tables, a grey made neutral with the white balance now renders neutral. The profile's hue/saturation map and look table apply after the white balance, at the chosen white, as the DNG specification orders them; before, they were applied at the as-shot white and a white-balance change re-balanced the colours they had already moved, so the grey kept a tint (on the Canon EOS R6 calibration, as-shot 5500 K set to 3200 K left a mid grey about 13 ΔE blue; on chart-made `.dcp` profiles, whose tables move colours further, about 25 ΔE). New photos get process V9; Update to Current Process moves edited photos to it. At the as-shot white V9 develops each pixel as before; previews of photos with a strong camera look can change slightly (about 1 ΔE on average on the Canon EOS R6 calibration).
