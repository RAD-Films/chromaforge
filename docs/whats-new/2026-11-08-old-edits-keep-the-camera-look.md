### Older edits keep their camera look
- Three fixes to the tone a raw takes from its camera's JPEG are part of process version 9:
  - midtones and shadows are no longer rendered darker than the camera;
  - a colourful dark frame no longer gets a steep step in its tone that showed as blotchy noise;
  - a dark frame from a Sony body in 12-bit uncompressed mode is no longer read on the wrong levels, which made it
    purple.
- Photos on an older process keep the look they had, even where it had one of these problems, so an edit you saved
  never changes under you, in the app or in an export. To get the fixes on a photo, update it to the current process.
