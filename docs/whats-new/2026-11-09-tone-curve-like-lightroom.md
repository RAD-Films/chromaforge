### Tone Curve like Lightroom Classic
- With the current process, a Tone Curve looks the same whether you export to sRGB, Display P3 or ProPhoto RGB. Before, a Red, Green or Blue curve turned out noticeably different in a ProPhoto export.
- Point curves follow Lightroom Classic's shape between points, and the RGB curve keeps colours' hue as Lightroom Classic's does, so curves from Lightroom Classic edits and presets look as they did there.
- The curve editor draws the same curve the photo gets.
